MOSAIC VOICES

This gallery brings together the stories and artwork created by youth through Mosaic Voices. Each panel reflects a unique experience, identity, or story, and together they form a collective mosaic exhibition at Fiddlesticks Community Centre. Visitors can explore the stories behind each piece through the artwork itself and optional written, audio, or video reflections shared by participants.
