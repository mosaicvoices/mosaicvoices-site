Behind the artwork

Sophia Willfang’s story

When you look into the mirror what do you see?
Do you see yourself or a version you hate?
I have those thoughts all the time. Some people see themselves, while others see what they think they are like. I used to look at myself and see a monster but slowly I have learnt to see myself truly. I love dark colors mixed with some light ones. My story to share is that “ see yourself as a human being”, others have this problem too. The different types of media I used are glitter, paint, markers, oil pastels, and water paint. My title is called “Who you truly are”. I want others to look into the mirror and see themselves for once. I loved coming here and making art.
