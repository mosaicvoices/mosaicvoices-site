MOSAIC VOICES

SMALL VOICES. BIG IMPACT.

Workshops combine guided reflection, hands-on art-making, and space for participants to decide how they want their story to be shared.

workshops

Sessions can be run as a single introduction, a short series, or a deeper residency, depending on the needs of the group and the space hosting the project.

intro session

A short workshop that introduces the mosaic concept, prompts a first story idea, and begins panel sketching.

studio session

A making-focused session where participants build their final panels and refine the visual language of their work.

story capture

Time set aside for recording audio, drafting written reflections, or filming short video responses that pair with the finished panel.

workshop setup

  • Designed for youth groups, classrooms, newcomer programs, and community hubs.
  • Can be adapted for different ages, literacy levels, and language supports.
  • Works best with space for conversation, art materials, and optional recording.
