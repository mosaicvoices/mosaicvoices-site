Behind the artwork

Parteek’s story

The Grinch holding a Christmas ornament represents my love for Christmas and my family’s strong connection to movies. Even though my family spends lots of time together, Christmas is special because everyone puts everything aside and meets up and spends a couple of days together. Those days are my favorite days when we all sit together, play games, and spend time with everyone, from young children to parents, older siblings, and grandparents. I make memories I will never forget. Since childhood, Christmas has been my favourite time of year because of the cozy feeling, snow-covered ground, family traditions, and memories we create together.
