GET INVOLVED

MOSAIC VOICES

Mosaic Voices is growing, and there are many ways to be part of it. Whether you're a young person looking to share your story, a volunteer wanting to support our workshops, or an organization interested in collaborating, we'd love to have you involved. Together, we can create more spaces for youth to express themselves, build connections, and have their voices seen and heard!

Interest Form

Ways to Get Involved

Youth Participants

  • Join the Mosaic Voices workshop series and become part of the community.
  • Explore your identity, experiences, and story through guided creative activities.
  • Create a personalized art panel that becomes part of the collective exhibition.
  • Share the story behind your piece in whatever way feels comfortable to you.

Community Partners

  • Help connect Mosaic Voices with youth who could benefit from the experience.
  • Support outreach and help bring the workshops to more young people.
  • Collaborate on future workshops, exhibitions, or community opportunities.

Support Mosaic Voices

  • Donate art materials or other supplies that can support our workshops.
  • Help us create and build elements of the Mosaic Voices exhibition.
  • Share Mosaic Voices with youth, families, schools, and organizations in your community.
