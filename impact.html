MOSAIC VOICES

SMALL VOICES. BIG IMPACT.

The impact of Mosaic Voices is both personal and public: participants gain confidence in telling their stories, and the community gains a richer, more visible record of youth experience.

impact

The project is meant to create cultural visibility, creative confidence, and stronger connections between young people and the places they live.

confidence

Participants practice shaping a story in their own words and presenting it in a form that feels meaningful to them.

representation

The mosaic makes youth perspectives visible in public space rather than leaving them hidden inside one-time workshops.

connection

Viewers encounter many distinct experiences in one shared display, which supports a stronger sense of mutual recognition across the community.

what success looks like

  • Young people see their stories reflected and respected.
  • The display continues to grow with new participants and new themes.
  • Community members engage with the work beyond a single event or workshop.
