HOW IT WORKS

MOSAIC VOICES

Mosaic Voices is a continuous creative journey. Through a series of guided workshops, participants explore their stories, discover and express their identities, build friendships, and strengthen their sense of belonging, all while creating something that becomes part of a larger community story.

1

DIVE IN:

Youth come together to explore identity, culture, belonging, and the experiences that have shaped who they are through guided conversations and creative activities.

CREATE:

Participants turn their ideas and stories into personalized art panels using colour, symbols, words, and different forms of creative expression. No art experience is needed.

2
3

SHARE:

Youth have the opportunity to share the story behind their piece in a way that feels comfortable to them, whether through writing, audio, video, or simply through their artwork.

COME TOGETHER:

Each participant's panel becomes part of the collective Mosaic Voices exhibition at Fiddlesticks Community Center, where individual stories come together in one shared display for the community to experience, reflect on, and celebrate.
