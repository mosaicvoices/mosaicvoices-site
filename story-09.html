Behind the artwork

Ava’s story

Growing into Myself explores how interests and experiences from my childhood have shaped who I am today. The pastel colors blending in the background show how my identity is ever-changing and unique. The objects and images represent different parts of myself that I consider important to who I am today. Through my artwork, I hope others get inspired to embrace their individuality and be proud of what makes them unique.
