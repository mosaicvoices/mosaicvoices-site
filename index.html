MOSAIC VOICES

SMALL VOICES. BIG IMPACT.

Mosaic Voices is a youth-led art and storytelling initiative that gives young people, especially newcomer, second-generation, and underrepresented youth, a space to explore and share their stories through creative expression. Participants create personalized art panels inspired by their own experiences and can share these stories behind them through written, audio, or video reflections. Each panel then becomes part of a collective mosaic display at FiddleSticks Community Centre, bringing different stories together in one shared space.

what we do
  • Youth create story-based art panels.
  • Panels are displayed behind a large acrylic cover at our community center.
  • QR codes link each panel to audio, video, or written stories.
  • Themes rotate monthly to keep the display fresh and meaningful.
